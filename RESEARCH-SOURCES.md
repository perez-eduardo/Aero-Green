# AeroGreen FBO research sources

These sources inform the prototype's Super Bowl-week planning context. The dashboard values are modeled assumptions for a demo, not official airport forecasts.

## Los Angeles: Super Bowl LVI, 2022

- FAA: [Super Bowl LVI flight requirements for general aviation pilots](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/super-bowl-lvi-flight-requirements-ga-pilots)
- The FAA described a February 6–14 reservation program covering LAX, VNY, BUR, LGB, HHR, SNA, ONT, and SBD, plus special procedures and a SoFi-centered TFR on game day.

## Phoenix: Super Bowl LVII, 2023

- FAA: [Ready for the Kickoff: What GA Pilots Need to Know about Super Bowl LVII](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/ready-kickoff-what-ga-pilots-need-know-about-super-bowl-lvii)
- The FAA used a reservation program across the Phoenix-area airport network, including PHX, DVT, GYR, IWA, SDL, GEU, CHD, FFZ, LAS, VGT, and HND.

## Las Vegas: Super Bowl LVIII, 2024

- FAA: [Super Bowl LVIII special traffic management procedures](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/domesticnotices/dom24001_sp.html)
- The FAA required parking and airspace slot reservations at LAS, HND, VGT, and BVU, including drop-and-go operations, and warned of substantial delays or possible denial without reservations.

## Current Los Angeles plan: Super Bowl LXI, 2027

- [Los Angeles Super Bowl Host Committee](https://lasuperbowlhc.com/)
- [LAX Area Advisory Committee minutes, February 12, 2026](https://www.lawa.org/sites/lawa/files/2026-05/LAX%20AAC%20Minutes%202026-2-12.pdf)
- Super Bowl LXI is confirmed for SoFi Stadium on February 14, 2027. The LAWA minutes identify Super Bowl 2027 as a future major event and describe major-event GA/PPR planning with FAA and NBAA coordination. Final 2027 FAA NOTAMs and event-specific airport procedures should be treated as pending until published.
