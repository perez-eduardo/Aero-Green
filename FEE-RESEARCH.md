# AeroGreen FBO fee research

## Published airport and FBO examples

### Van Nuys (VNY)

- LAWA/VNY FY2026–27 landing fee: **$6.44 per 1,000 lb maximum landing weight**, billed on arrival; touch-and-go operations are billed.
- Example for a Gulfstream G650 at 83,500 lb MLW: approximately **$538 per landing**.
- Clay Lacy VNY publicly lists example large-aircraft FBO rates: **$850 ramp handling**, **$70 facility fee**, **$50 security fee**, **$250 quiet-hour fee**, and **$9.08 per gallon Jet-A**. These are one FBO's published rates, not a universal VNY price.
- Sources: [VNY landing fees](https://www.iflyvny.com/airport-facts/vny-landing-fees) and [Clay Lacy VNY rates](https://www.claylacy.com/contact-us/locations/van-nuys-airport-los-angeles-kvny/).

### Los Angeles International (LAX)

- LAWA general-aviation landing fee effective March 1, 2026: **$7.85 per 1,000 lb MLW**; billed upon arrival; touch-and-go operations are billed.
- Example for a G650 at 83,500 lb MLW: approximately **$655 per landing**.
- LAWA states FBO charges are separate from the airport landing-fee invoice. Add FBO handling, parking or hangar, fuel, security, transportation, and any event-period terms separately.
- Source: [LAWA General Aviation Fees at LAX](https://www.lawa.org/groups-and-divisions/operations-and-emergency-management/airport-and-ground-transportation-permits/airfield-permits/air-carrier-operating-permit/general-aviation-fees-lax).

### Ontario (ONT)

- Ontario International Airport Authority FY2026–27 non-signatory landing rate for aircraft over 25,000 lb: **$2.11 per 1,000 lb maximum gross landing weight**.
- Example for a G650 at 83,500 lb: approximately **$176 per landing**.
- Public non-diversion aircraft parking is listed at **$0.81 per 1,000 lb per 24 hours**, but ONT rules say general-aviation service and overnight parking are normally handled on FBO leasehold property. Confirm the FBO quote before using public-parking math.
- Source: [ONT FY2026–27 rates and charges](https://www.flyontario.com/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-0625_agenda_packet_-_public_0.pdf) and [ONT rules and regulations](https://www.flyontario.com/security-operations/ont-rules-regulations).

## Fee categories the prototype should track

1. Airport landing fee, usually based on MLW or MGLW.
2. Touch-and-go or drop-and-go landing charge.
3. FBO ramp or handling fee.
4. Facility or terminal fee.
5. Security or access fee.
6. Quiet-hour, after-hours, or overtime fee.
7. Overnight ramp parking or hangar fee.
8. Tow, GPU, lavatory, potable water, catering, and other service charges.
9. Jet-A fuel, fuel-flowage fee, minimum fuel purchase, or contract-fuel terms.
10. SAF premium, if requested.
11. Ground transportation, vehicle staging, or airport trip fees.
12. Customs or CBP user fees for international operations, when applicable.
13. Event-period PPR/slot administration, premium handling, overflow repositioning, or minimum-stay charges.
14. Taxes, credit-card processing, and invoice/administration fees.

## Event-fee rule for the demo

No official Super Bowl LXI 2027 event surcharge was found in the public airport sources reviewed. The prototype therefore shows **Quote required** for event fees rather than inventing a price. The FBO must confirm the event-period terms, and operators should also check the FAA NOTAM and airport reservation/PPR instructions when published.
